Rope Border Options

Woven Border

Standard border option. 5/16 Polyester rope hand woven through each individual netting mesh along the entire perimeter of the netting and secured at each corner via heavy-duty UV treated zip tie.

Sewn Border

Recommended border option. 5/16 Polyester rope machine sewn and lock stitched (each stitch approx. every 1 cm) around the entire perimeter.
